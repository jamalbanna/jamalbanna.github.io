      The short version

      I write software for a living and run organizations alongside it, at the same time. Right now that is senior software engineer at Do360 Digital Construction in Beirut, co-founder, CTO and CFO of DevNov, Country Manager of AIESEC in Lebanon, and two roles with AIESEC's global team, including oversight of its CAD 280,000 Global Information Systems fund.

      It started further from software than it sounds. I studied mechatronics engineering at the University of Balamand, worked the counter and the kitchen at Burger King in Aley, then moved to computer science at Haigazian University, where I resumed the Computer Science Student Society after years of it being stopped.

      The pattern since then: build the thing, then teach the people around it. I have been a tutor, a lab instructor at the American University of Beirut, and the project manager who upskilled 4 of my CURA teammates into project managers. At AIESEC in Lebanon it meant growing the membership from 4 to 60 active members.
