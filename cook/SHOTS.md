# Shots

A shot is one screen state at a fixed viewport. Capture with `node cook/tools/shoot.mjs <outDir>`.

| Shot | Ref | Shows | Viewport | Take from it | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S01 | R01-parallel.png | Hero, playhead at today | 1440x900 | branch graph, wide name, readout panel, palette | chart height on short laptops |
| S02 | none | Full page, desktop | 1440 wide, full height | rhythm of dark, light, dark, vermilion chapters | spacing between sections |
| S03 | none | Hero, phone | 390x844 at 2x | compact graph, name, readout | readout list below the fold |
| S04 | none | Full page, phone | 390 wide, full height | single column, tap targets | long experience section |
